# 09 · Learning & Community

Where to keep learning after this repo, and the communities that make medical-device security a field rather than a solo grind. Curated, not exhaustive, quality over quantity.

---

## Books

- **Practical IoT Hacking** - Chantzis, Stais, Calderón, Deirmentzoglou, Woods (No Starch). Includes medical-device-oriented chapters (DICOM, etc.); the best single hands-on IoT/embedded foundation for this work.
- **The Hardware Hacking Handbook** - van Woudenberg & O'Flynn (No Starch). Deep on embedded attacks, fault injection, side channels - the hardware half of device testing.
- **The IoT Hacker's Handbook** - Aditya Gupta. UART/JTAG/SPI/BLE/ZigBee/firmware fundamentals.
- **Medical Device Cybersecurity for Engineers and Manufacturers** - Wirth, Gates, Smith (Artech House). The defender's/manufacturer's view - invaluable context for a tester.
- **Do No Harm: Protecting Connected Medical Devices…** - Matthew Fisher. The regulatory/privacy/threat-landscape picture for hospitals and devices.
- **Gray Hat Hacking: The Ethical Hacker's Handbook** - broad offensive reference for building base skills.

## Courses & training with real labs

- **Hardwear.io - Medical Device Hacking and Defense** (teardown → RF → mobile/cloud → PoC). <https://hardwear.io/>
- **DEF CON Biohacking Village** workshops (Medical Device Hacking 101/201) and the Device Lab. <https://www.villageb.io/>
- **Offensive IoT / embedded courses** (e.g. vendor-neutral IoT exploitation, ARM/embedded RE) to build transferable base skills - see providers referenced by the community.
- **PortSwigger Web Security Academy** (free) for the web/API half. <https://portswigger.net/web-security>
- **OWASP MASTG** self-study for mobile. <https://mas.owasp.org/>

## Conferences & villages (watch the talks even if you can't attend)

- **DEF CON Biohacking Village** - the center of gravity for this community. Talks on the DEF CON media server.
- **Hardwear.io** (USA/Netherlands) - hardware & device security, with medical tracks.
- **Black Hat** - regular medical-device research.
- **TROOPERS** (Germany) - strong embedded/health research (ERNW community).
- **HIMSS** - the healthcare-IT/industry side; useful for context and stakeholder view.
- **S4 / ICS-focused events** - OT/ICS overlaps heavily with hospital device security.

## Organizations & communities to plug into

- **I Am The Cavalry** - grassroots public-safety security community; authors of the Hippocratic Oath for Connected Medical Devices; runs the BHV Device Lab with the village. <https://iamthecavalry.org/>
- **DEF CON Biohacking Village** - year-round community, not just at the con. <https://www.villageb.io/>
- **MDIC (Medical Device Innovation Consortium)** - public-private; co-authored the threat-modeling playbook and CVD/pentest reports. <https://mdic.org/>
- **H-ISAC (Health Information Sharing and Analysis Center)** - threat intel & sharing for the sector. <https://health-isac.org/>
- **HSCC Cybersecurity Working Group** - the consensus guidance body (JSP2, HIC-MaLTS, etc.). <https://healthsectorcouncil.org/>
- **Archimedes / Secure Medicine (Univ. of Michigan, Kevin Fu)** - academic medical-device security, runs research courses. <https://www.secure-medicine.org/>
- **FDA Digital Health / medical device cybersecurity** program pages - the regulator's own resources. <https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence>

## Feeds to follow weekly

- **CISA ICS Medical Advisories (ICSMA)** - the running vuln feed. <https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories>
- **FDA cybersecurity safety communications.** <https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/safety-communications>
- **NVD / OSV** - component & vendor searches to pair with SBOMs. <https://nvd.nist.gov/> · <https://osv.dev/>
- **MedCrypt** and other vendor research blogs - practical device-security writing (read critically; vendors have products to sell).
- **ERNW / insinuator.net** - deep technical write-ups (e.g. the ManiMed series). <https://insinuator.net/>

## People & research lineages worth studying

Not an endorsement list, just names whose *public research* teaches the field (look up their talks/papers):
- **Kevin Fu** - academic foundations of medical-device security (pacemaker/ICD research, Archimedes).
- **Billy Rios & Jonathan Butts** - infusion pumps, pacemaker ecosystems.
- **Jerome Radcliffe** and the late **Barnaby Jack** - early insulin-pump/implant demonstrations that put this on the map.
- **Marie Moe** - pacemaker-patient-turned-researcher; the human side of implant security.
- **Beau Woods, Joshua Corman** - I Am The Cavalry, policy & the Hippocratic Oath.
- **Scott Gayou** - the Medfusion 4000 end-to-end research ([07](07-case-studies-and-cves.md)).
- The **ERNW/ManiMed** researchers (e.g. Oliver Matula) - methodical device assessments.

## Related community resources

- **awesome-medsec** - a community link list of tools, talks, and papers; a good complementary breadth resource to this repo's depth. <https://github.com/zseha/awesome-medsec>
- **OWASP** projects: MASTG/MASVS, FSTM, API Security Top 10, WSTG, IoT, and the Secure Medical Device Deployment Standard. <https://owasp.org/>
- **MITRE healthcare cyber** resources. <https://healthcyber.mitre.org/>
