# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful, be helpful, assume good faith. This is a small, collaborative field and we keep it welcoming to newcomers.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of experience level or background.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Help people who are just getting started. We were all there once.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sharing working exploits against fielded medical devices, or anything that puts patients at risk.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Encouraging unlawful or unauthorized testing.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel tied to the repository.

## Enforcement

If you see behaviour that breaks these rules, open an issue or contact the maintainer, [@AnanthapadmanabhanA](https://github.com/AnanthapadmanabhanA). Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may block repeat offenders.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). Kept intentionally short.
